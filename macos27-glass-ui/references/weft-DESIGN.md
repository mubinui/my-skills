# Design System: Weft

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Pro App Window"**

Weft is built the way a Mac pro tool is built, in the line of Xcode and Instruments, not the way a web dashboard is. The window is the frame, drawn as macOS 27 ("Golden Gate") draws it. A Liquid Glass sidebar runs flush to the window's leading edge, full height, tinted by a soft desktop that shows only through it. A unified frosted toolbar band lies across the top of the content, and the content scrolls beneath it. The toolbar's controls are Liquid Glass capsules, with a centred activity view. Content sits on an opaque window background (white, or near-black in dark mode), and an inspector sits beside it. The two canvases, Studio and Explore, are the exception in layout, not in material: the canvas takes the full width, and its panes and controls float over it as Liquid Glass. Density follows AppKit: a 13px body, 28px controls, hairline dividers, and tables rather than walls of cards.

Colour is rationed. The accent is Graphite, the monochrome macOS accent, so the primary action is the darkest thing on a light window and the lightest on a dark one. Hue is left to status, where it carries meaning, to the lanes that code pipeline stages, and to the sidebar icons, one hue per place, as macOS 27 colours them. Translucency belongs only to chrome: the sidebar, the toolbar band and its capsules, menus, popovers, the build log, toasts, and the panes and capsules that float over a canvas. Liquid Glass has a darkened outer edge, a bright specular rim and a sheen where the light lands. Glass marks a surface that floats over the work, not a finish applied to everything.

This build departs from its direction contract in six places, and the build wins in each. The contract named system blue as the only accent; the user rejected blue during the build, and orange and brown anywhere, so the accent is Graphite. The contract put a page title at the toolbar's leading edge; the finish review removed it on every page, so pages are named by their large title or the sidebar selection. The contract named orange for needs-attention; it is yellow. The contract gave dark grouped boxes as #2C2C2E; the build ships a slightly deeper value (see the grouped-dark token). The contract's sidebar was inset 8px with an 18px radius; the user moved to the macOS 27 look, so the sidebar is flush and square to the window edge. The contract tinted sidebar icons with the accent; they now carry one hue per place.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Apple system semantics in both light and dark themes, plus Match system.
- A monochrome Graphite accent. Colour means status, pipeline lane or sidebar place, nothing else.
- Every status has a shape as well as a hue: a filled disc, a ring, a diamond, a thin ring, or a spinning ring.
- Grouped boxes are a step of grey on the window, with no border and no shadow.
- A flush Liquid Glass sidebar over a soft desktop, and a frosted toolbar band that content scrolls beneath.
- Liquid Glass carries a darkened edge, a specular rim and a top sheen. Content is opaque.
- Canvases take the full width; the stage palette, the inspector, the node detail and the canvas controls float over them as glass.
- Wires are neutral grey. Colour on a link is reserved for a link that crosses sources or graphs.
- The system font stack for everything; SF Mono only for code and data.

## Colors

A near-monochrome Apple-grey palette. Three status hues, four lane hues and nine place hues are the only saturated colour, and none of them is blue, orange or brown.

### Primary
- **Graphite** (graphite / graphite-dark): the accent. It fills the default push button, the Build segment of Studio's toolbar group, the highlighted menu row, a hovered citation marker, and the build-progress hairline. It inverts per theme: ink on light, near-white on dark. Because the accent reads as text, links are told apart by an underline (line-strong, going to currentColor on hover), not by a hue.

### Secondary
- **Ready Green** (ready / ready-dark, with ready-wash): grounded, built, current. The switch's on track, ready badges, the filled-disc glyph and ready meters.
- **Attention Yellow** (attention / attention-dark, with attention-wash): needs attention, rebuild needed, busy. It appears only as a glyph (a ring, or the spinning ring's arc), a badge tint and a meter fill. In light mode yellow is never text: the attention text token maps to the ink colour, and yellow text is allowed only on dark.
- **Refusal Red** (error / error-dark, with error-wash): refused, failed, needs fixes, destructive. The diamond glyph, error badges, field errors, the destructive ghost button, and the wash behind inline errors and refusals.

### Tertiary
- **Lane colours**: Ingest Teal, Index Violet, Query Magenta, Answer Graphite (lane-*). These are data coding for pipeline stages only: the lane-tinted icon tile on each stage card and in the stage palette (the icon in the lane colour on a 15% wash of it). Canvas wires and lane bands do not carry lane colour. They never mark state or action.
- **Place hues** (hue-*): one hue per sidebar destination. Graphs teal, Sources green, Connections violet, Studio magenta, Ask cyan-teal, Explore purple, Monitor pink, API grey, Advisor graphite. Deeper in light, brighter in dark. They colour the sidebar row's icon and nothing else.
- **Desktop fields** (wall-a / wall-b / wall-c): three low-alpha radial fields (teal, violet, pink) painted on the window behind the sidebar. They are the "wallpaper" that the sidebar glass picks up, and nothing else shows them.
- **Node ramp** (`--node-1` to `--node-10`, in the sidecar): the categorical palette for graph nodes in Explore. It has no blue, no orange and no brown. Hues are deeper in light and brighter in dark. It colours the nodes and the links that cross from one source or graph to another; links inside one graph are wire grey.

### Neutral
- **Ink / Muted / Dim** (ink, ink-muted, ink-dim and their dark twins): primary text; secondary text and control labels; hints, column headers, section labels and idle glyphs.
- **Window** (window / window-dark): the opaque content background, the main column and the table background.
- **Grouped** (grouped / grouped-dark): grouped boxes, the inspector, stage rows, the summary strip.
- **Raised** (raised / raised-dark): text fields, a selected segment, rows nested inside a grouped box.
- **Pane** (pane / pane-dark): opaque side panes beside the content, such as the Connections list. The Studio palette and inspector and the Explore detail no longer use it; they float as glass.
- **Liquid Glass** (lg-fill, lg-edge, lg-spec, lg-sheen): the lens material of toolbar capsules, the activity view and the capsules over a canvas (canvas stats, legend, zoom controls, Explore's Fit/Relax group). Edge is the darkened outer line, spec the bright inner rim, sheen the light that lands on the top-left.
- **Sidebar glass and selection** (sidebar-glass, sidebar-selection): the sidebar's translucent tint, and the grey pill of its current row.
- **Toolbar band** (toolbar-band): the frosted band across the top of the content.
- **Fills** (fill, fill-hover, fill-active): the tinted fill of push buttons, badges, the segmented track and hovers inside glass. Black-alpha on light, white-alpha on dark.
- **Lines** (line, line-strong, field-border): hairline dividers, table rules, field strokes, the toolbar band's lower hairline.
- **Wire** (wire / wire-dark): the neutral grey of every Studio wire, connection-handle ring and legend swatch, and of Explore links inside one graph.
- **Selection** (selection / selection-dark, selection-blur): table selection. It is mid-grey with white text only while the table has focus, and a quiet grey tint when it does not.

### Named Rules
**The Graphite Rule.** The accent is monochrome. Never introduce system blue, orange or brown as an accent, a link colour, a selection or a focus ring. The user rejected all three.

**The Status Has a Shape Rule.** Status is never hue alone. Ready is a filled disc, attention a ring, error a diamond (a square rotated 45°), idle a thin ring, and busy a spinning ring. A new status surface uses the status glyph or the badge dot, never a bare coloured dot.

**The Yellow Is Never Text Rule.** On a light window, yellow is a glyph, a tint or a meter. The words beside it are ink.

**The Lanes Are Data Rule.** Lane colours code pipeline stages and nothing else. They never signal success, error or selection.

**The Neutral Wire Rule.** A wire inside one pipeline or one graph is wire grey, 1.5px in Studio. The chosen stage's or node's links turn ink and everything else steps back; a link never takes a lane or node hue unless it crosses sources or graphs in Explore.

**The Place Hue Rule.** A place hue colours only that place's sidebar icon. The row's text stays ink, its selection stays a grey pill, and a disabled row's icon falls back to muted. A new destination takes a new hue from the same no-blue, no-orange, no-brown family, with a light and a dark value.

## Typography

**Display Font:** SF Pro via the system stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, falling back to Segoe UI Variable, system-ui, Helvetica Neue and Arial)
**Body Font:** the same system stack
**Label/Mono Font:** SF Mono via ui-monospace, for code and data only

**Character:** This is the macOS text ramp, used as AppKit uses it. There are no web fonts; the build removed them. Weight and size carry hierarchy. Tracking is slightly negative and tightens as size grows.

### Hierarchy
- **Large Title** (700, 28px, 1.15, -0.022em; 24px on phones): one per scrolling page. The page names itself here, not in the toolbar.
- **Title** (700, 22px, 1.2): the heading of a major pane or section.
- **Title 2** (600, 17px, 1.3): sheet titles and panel headings.
- **Headline** (600, 13px): row titles, stage names in lists, the inspector status line. A stage card's title on the canvas is one step larger (600, 14px, -0.012em) with a 12px dim configuration line, so it still reads at about 12px at the fitted zoom.
- **Body** (400, 13px, 1.46): all interface text and controls.
- **Reading** (400, 15px, 1.62, max 72ch): generated answers, the one long-read surface.
- **Callout** (400, 12px): hints, row subtitles, the activity status text.
- **Section Label** (600, 11px, sentence case, dim): section headers, table column headers, sidebar group labels, figure captions, canvas lane-band labels ("Ingest and index", "Query and answer").
- **Figure** (600, 22px, tabular numerals): headline counts in the inspector, the summary strip and canvas stats. A zero drops to dim at weight 500.
- **Mono** (11.5px, tabular): IDs, model names, code, config values.
- **Graph label** (600, 11.5px, on a glass pill): node names on the Explore canvas, held at 11.5px on screen at every zoom.

### Named Rules
**The Sentence Case Rule.** Section labels are 11px semibold in sentence case, never tracked capitals. There are no uppercase mono eyebrows.

**The Mono Is Data Rule.** SF Mono only for values a machine produced or reads: IDs, code, model strings, numbers in data cells. Never for labels or headings.

## Layout

The window is a flex row. First is the sidebar, flush to the window's leading edge and full height, var(--sidebar-w) wide (232px). It has no inset and no corner radius. Then comes the main column, opaque window, with the toolbar laid over its top and the content filling it.

The toolbar is one frosted band, 60px tall (var(--toolbar-h)), positioned over the top of the main column so the content scrolls beneath it. It is a three-column grid (1fr · auto · 1fr) with 18px leading and 14px trailing padding and no page title. The leading cell holds only the navigation menu button, and only on phones. The centre holds the activity view, a 36px capsule clamp(300px, 36vw, 520px) wide. The trailing cell holds the toolbar controls 10px apart. Outside Studio: a Build capsule, then a More (⋯) icon capsule. In Studio: one grouped capsule holding Save and Build, then More.

Because the band covers the top, every screen starts beneath it. Scrolling pages pad their top by the toolbar height plus 20px (80px), their sides by 32px and their bottom by 56px, with scroll padding of one toolbar height and a 1200px max inner width. Screens that are not scrolling pages (Studio, Explore, Connections) pad their top by exactly the toolbar height. Sticky elements, such as the inspector, stick at the toolbar height plus 12px.

The Graphs reference surface is a table plus a 340px sticky inspector with a 20px gap. It collapses to one column below 1100px. Card grids use 14px gaps.

Studio is one full-width canvas under a shape bar. The shape bar (window ground, a bottom hairline, 8px 16px padding) holds the Canvas/Stages segmented control, a "Stages" push button that toggles the palette, a separator and the template shape; there is no separate toolbar row. Over the canvas float two glass panes, each inset 12px from the canvas edges and top to bottom. The stage palette (252px) sits on the left, is closed by default and slides in from the left. The inspector (340px, 310px below 1280px) sits on the right, exists only while a stage is selected, has a close button, and slides in from the right. When the chosen stage would sit under the inspector, the canvas pans left at the same zoom; it never re-zooms. Framing uses per-side pixel padding: 64px top, 56px bottom, 36px right, and 36px left (288px with the palette open). Floating chrome sits 14px from the canvas edges: the index figures top-right, the legend bottom-right, the zoom controls bottom-left.

Explore is one full-width graph. Its node detail floats on the right as the same 340px glass pane as the Studio inspector; the colour legend is a glass pane bottom-left and the Fit/Relax group a glass capsule bottom-right, both 14px from the edges.

Responsive steps: below 1280px the sidebar narrows to 204px. Below 1024px it becomes a 68px icon rail (36px rows, 18px icons, 10px group labels, the graph name hidden). Studio opens on the Stages list, and choosing a stage replaces the list with the inspector as a plain, opaque, full-width pane with an "All stages" back button; the canvas stats and legend hide. The Explore detail becomes a static full-width pane below the graph (up to 50vh, a top hairline). Below 768px the sidebar hides and becomes a drawer from the left, the activity view keeps only the graph pop-up, optional table columns hide, and pages pad the toolbar height plus 14px on top and 16px at the sides. On coarse pointers, buttons grow to 40px (34px small) and fields to 40px at 16px text.

Spacing rhythm: 4 / 8 / 14 / 18 / 22 / 32px. Controls pack at 6 to 10px gaps; sections sit 14 to 20px apart.

## Elevation & Depth

This is a hybrid system: flat content with tonal layering, and glass and shadow reserved for chrome. Grouped boxes sit a step of grey off the window, with no border and no shadow. Wells recess by a translucent tint. On the Studio canvas, stage cards are lit raised surfaces (raised with a top-left sheen, the darkened edge, the specular rim and a soft drop), because they sit on a free canvas, not in a layout. Lane bands behind them are flat: a 3% ink tint with a 5% ink inner line.

Chrome is Liquid Glass. The sidebar is the heaviest glass (a 50px blur at 200% saturation, with a top sheen gradient), and it is tinted by the desktop fields behind it. Its trailing edge is a darkened line with a specular line just inside it. The toolbar band is a lighter frost (a 24px blur at 180% saturation over a fading band gradient) closed by a 0.5px hairline. Toolbar capsules and the activity view are lenses (an 18px blur at 190% saturation), drawn with a 0.5px darkened edge, a 1px specular rim along the top, a sheen at the top-left and a soft lift. Menus, popovers, the build log, toasts and the phone nav drawer use the same edge, rim and sheen over popover glass, with a 30 to 40px blur and the floating shadow. The floating canvas panes (the Studio palette and inspector, the Explore detail) are the same popover glass at a 34px blur with a 22px radius. Canvas capsules (stats, legend, zoom controls, Fit/Relax) are lenses like the toolbar capsules. When backdrop-filter is unsupported, popover glass and the sidebar fall back to the opaque solid colour.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hairline lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.06)`): small opaque tiles that sit proud of a pane, such as template cards at rest.
- **Lifted** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.06), 0 10px 26px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.16)`): a chosen template card.
- **Stage rest** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 .5px var(--lg-edge), inset 0 1px 0 var(--lg-spec), 0 2px 10px -3px rgba(0,0,0,.12)`): a stage card at rest. On hover the drop deepens to `0 8px 22px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.24)` and the card lifts 1px.
- **Stage selected** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--text), 0 10px 26px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.3)`): the chosen stage, a 2px ink ring. An erroring stage takes a 1.5px refusal-red ring (2px when also selected).
- **Floating** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 .5px rgba(0,0,0,.10), 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.06), 0 24px 56px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.26)`): menus, popovers, the build log, sheets, the nav drawer, toasts, and the floating canvas panes.
- **Liquid Glass ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 .5px var(--lg-edge), inset 0 1px 0 var(--lg-spec)`): every Liquid Glass capsule and floating glass surface, before its lift or floating shadow.
- **Liquid lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px -6px rgba(0,0,0,.16)`): toolbar capsules, the activity view and canvas capsules.
- **Sidebar edge** (`box-shadow: inset -1px 0 0 var(--lg-spec), 1px 0 0 var(--lg-edge)`): the flush sidebar's trailing edge.
- **Toolbar hairline** (`box-shadow: 0 .5px 0 var(--line)`): the bottom of the toolbar band.
- **Button glow** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 1px rgba(0,0,0,.10)`): the primary push button.
- Dark mode deepens every shadow and darkens the glass edge (see the sidecar).

### Named Rules
**The Glass Is Chrome Rule.** Translucency and blur belong only to surfaces that float over the work or frame it: the sidebar, the toolbar band, toolbar capsules, the activity view, menus, popovers, the build log, toasts, the nav drawer, and the panes and capsules floating over a canvas. Content panes, tables and grouped boxes are opaque, and a canvas pane that stops floating (below 1024px) drops its glass.

**The Wallpaper Behind Glass Rule.** The desktop fields are painted on the window behind the sidebar, and the main column covers them with opaque window. The wallpaper is seen only through the sidebar glass, never behind content.

**The Darkened Edge Rule.** A Liquid Glass surface is outlined by a 0.5px darkened edge and lit by a 1px specular rim inside its top, with a sheen at the top-left. A plain grey hairline alone is not Liquid Glass.

**The Step of Grey Rule.** A grouped box is told from the window by tone alone. It gets no border and no drop shadow.

## Shapes

The shapes are soft, Apple-continuous rectangles and capsules, a step rounder in macOS 27. Push buttons, toolbar capsules, the toolbar group, the activity view, badges, the segmented control, toasts and switches are full capsules (999px). Text fields and pop-up buttons use 8px. Sidebar rows, menu rows and the inspector's row groups use 10px. Wells, inline errors, notices, tables and stage rows use 12px; a stage card's icon tile uses 10px. Menus, the issue list and stage cards use 16px. Grouped boxes, the Graphs inspector and the build log use 18px. Floating canvas panes use 22px. Sheets, the nav drawer and canvas lane bands use 24px. The sidebar itself is square: it runs flush to the window edge. Selected table rows round only their outer ends (8px). Borders are rare: only text fields and hairline dividers draw a stroke; stage cards are outlined by the Liquid Glass edge, not a border. Badges are tinted, never outlined. Pop-up buttons carry the macOS up-down chevrons.

## Components

### Buttons
Capsule push buttons with a tinted fill. The default action is the solid one.
- **Shape:** full capsule, 28px tall (24px small, 36px in the toolbar).
- **Push:** the fill tint with ink text, 13px at weight 500. Hover and press step through fill-hover and fill-active over 150ms.
- **Primary:** a solid Graphite capsule with inverse text and a 1px glow. Use one per view, for the view's next action (Rebuild in the inspector, Build in Studio).
- **Ghost:** transparent with muted text, taking the fill on hover. **Danger** is the ghost with red text and a red wash on hover.
- **Toolbar:** a 36px Liquid Glass capsule (16px side padding; 36px square for icon-only, such as More). It scales to 1.03 on hover and 0.97 on press over 200ms on the exponential ease-out.
- **Toolbar group:** related toolbar actions share one 36px glass capsule; Explore's Fit and Relax share one over the graph. In Studio, Save and Build sit in it as 30px segments; Save is transparent until hovered, and Build is the tinted Graphite segment with an inner top sheen.
- **Disabled:** 40% opacity.

### Chips (badges)
- **Style:** a small capsule (11px, weight 500, 2px 8px padding), tinted and never outlined. Neutral badges use the fill with muted text.
- **Status:** ready, attention and error badges tint with their wash, and their dot takes the status shape. The attention badge keeps ink text in light mode.
- **Lane badge:** currentColor at 13% as its background.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 18px for grouped boxes and the Graphs inspector, 12px for stage rows.
- **Background:** grouped. Rows nested inside a grouped box use raised.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none. Stage cards on the canvas are the exception (see Stage Card).
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 14 to 22px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** raised background, a 1px field-border stroke, 8px radius, 28px minimum height, 4px 9px padding.
- **Focus:** the border shifts to 45% ink, with a 3px focus band. The global focus ring is a 3px graphite band offset 1px, never blue.
- **Error:** a red border, and a 12px red message below.
- **Pop-up button:** the field shape with up-down chevrons at the trailing edge.
- **Switch:** a 38×20 capsule track (fill-active off, ready green on) with a white 22×16 capsule knob that always casts a small shadow and slides 12px, and an On/Off word beside it.
- **Segmented control:** a fill-tinted capsule track. The chosen segment is lit glass: raised, with a top-left sheen, the darkened edge, the specular rim and a small drop, at weight 500.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** a flush Liquid Glass column, full height, with the Weft mark, then a Workspace group (Graphs, Sources, Connections), then the open graph's group under a "This graph" label with the graph name.
- **Rows:** 32px tall, 13px, 10px radius. Each icon carries its place hue. Hover takes the fill. The current row takes the sidebar-selection grey pill with an inner top sheen, at weight 600; its icon keeps its hue. Selection is a grey pill, never coloured. Disabled rows drop to 38% with a muted icon.
- **Menus:** Liquid Glass popovers, 16px radius and 6px padding, with 30px rows at 10px radius. A highlighted row is solid Graphite with inverse text, as a Mac menu is.
- **Mobile:** below 768px the sidebar becomes a 300px glass drawer from the left (8px from the screen edge, 24px radius), with 44px rows at 15px.

### Activity View (signature)
One 36px Liquid Glass capsule at the centre of the toolbar reports which graph is open, what state its index is in, and a build's progress, the way Xcode does. It holds three things. First, a borderless graph pop-up at weight 600. Then a hairline separator, a status glyph and 12px status text. While building, the glyph is a spinning ring and a 2px ink hairline fills along the bottom edge (transform scaleX, 500ms ease-out). Clicking the status opens the build log, a glass popover (18px radius) hanging just below the toolbar band that scales in from 0.97. An "Edited" note appears when there are unsaved changes.

### Stage Card (Studio canvas)
A 220x64 lit raised surface with a 16px radius: raised with a top-left sheen, the Liquid Glass edge and rim, and a soft drop. It holds a 32px icon tile (10px radius) carrying the stage's lane colour on a 15% wash with a 30% inner line, a 14px semibold title, a 12px dim one-line configuration (ellipsised), and the shared status glyph at the trailing edge (ok disc, warn ring, bad diamond). Hover lifts it 1px and deepens the drop over 200ms on the exponential ease-out. Selection is a 2px ink ring; an error is a refusal-red ring. Connection handles are 9px window-coloured discs with a 1.5px wire ring, hidden until the card is hovered; a hovered handle fills ink and scales 1.25.

### Canvas Wires and Lanes
Wires are smoothstep paths with a 16px corner radius, 1.5px wire grey, ending in a small closed arrowhead (14px). Wires that read the index are dashed (5 6). When a stage is selected, its wires turn ink at 2px and all others drop to 35% opacity over 200ms. Two soft bands (24px radius, 3% ink tint, 5% ink inner line) sit behind the two halves of the pipeline, sized to the stages present, and are labelled "Ingest and index" and "Query and answer" in the section-label style at their top-left. The canvas ground is a 22px dot grid.

### Canvas Chrome
Everything over a canvas is glass. The index figures are one 40px capsule, top-right: each figure 14px semibold tabular with an 11px dim caption, divided by hairlines. The legend is a 32px capsule, bottom-right, in 11.5px muted: a solid and a dashed wire swatch ("Ingestion path", "Reads the index"), plus "Click a stage to configure it" after a hairline while nothing is selected. Zoom controls are a vertical capsule of 30px round buttons. There is no minimap.

### Floating Pane
The stage palette, the Studio inspector and the Explore node detail share one material: popover glass at a 34px blur and 190% saturation, a top-left sheen, the darkened edge, the specular rim and the floating shadow, with a 22px radius, inset 12px from the canvas edges. Each slides in from its own side (16px, scaling from 0.985, 240 to 260ms on the exponential ease-out). Palette rows are 34px at 10px radius with a 26px lane-tinted icon tile; a stage already in the graph reads in ink with an "In graph" note, one not yet used reads dim with a faded tile and can be dragged.

### Explore Graph (canvas 2D)
Things are flat discs in their node-ramp colour with a rim of the window colour, so touching nodes stay distinct; there is no lit-sphere cap and no vignette. Passages are outlined rounded squares (30% corner) with a 22% fill of their colour. Links inside one graph are wire grey; only links that cross sources or graphs keep a blend of their two colours, and links between graphs are dashed. A selected node takes a 2px ink ring, and its links draw in ink at 60% opacity and 1.3px while unrelated nodes drop to 13%. Labels are 11.5px semibold on glass pills with a hairline edge, placed most-important-first and skipped where one would overlap. The colour legend is a glass pane (18px radius) bottom-left; Fit and Relax share a glass capsule bottom-right.

### Status Glyph
A 9px mark: a filled sage disc (ready), a 2.25px yellow ring (attention), a 1.5px dim ring (idle), a red diamond (error), or a spinning yellow-arc ring (busy). It is used in the activity view, table status cells, the inspector and on stage cards. Badge dots follow the same shapes at 7px.

### Table (macOS list view)
The table sits in a 12px box drawn by a 1px line ring on the window. Headers are 11px semibold dim sentence case over a hairline. Cells have 9px 12px padding, with a zebra stripe at 2% ink. Row titles are headline weight with a 12px dim subtitle. Numeric columns are right-aligned with tabular numerals. Hovered rows take the fill. A selected row is selection-blur until the table has focus, then graphite grey with white text. Keyboard focus rings the whole row with an inset focus band.

### Inspector
A sticky grouped box (20px padding, 16px gaps, 18px radius) beside the table, sticking just below the toolbar band. It holds the selected item's title and description, a status line, one primary button for the next action, a figure strip with hairline dividers, raised row groups, and "Open in" push buttons.

### Motion
Controls transition in 120 to 150ms. Toolbar capsules scale 1.03 on hover and 0.97 on press over 200ms. Popovers, sheets, the switch knob and the segmented selection take 180 to 220ms on an exponential ease-out (cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)). Popovers and sheets scale from 0.97 and fade in; they never slide. The floating canvas panes are the one exception: they belong to a side, so they slide in 16px from it (scaling from 0.985, 240 to 260ms). Canvas wires fade and turn ink over 200ms; stage cards lift over 200ms. Reduced motion collapses all durations.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the accent Graphite: the ink colour on light, near-white on dark. The primary button is the darkest (or lightest) thing on the window.
- **Do** pair every status hue with its shape: a disc, a ring, a diamond, a thin ring, or a spinning ring.
- **Do** keep attention text in ink on light windows, with yellow only in the glyph, the badge tint or the meter.
- **Do** separate grouped boxes from the window by a step of grey (grouped on window), with no border and no shadow.
- **Do** run the sidebar flush to the window edge, full height, closed by the darkened edge and specular line.
- **Do** start every screen beneath the 60px toolbar band: scrolling pages at the toolbar height plus 20px, other screens at the toolbar height.
- **Do** draw Liquid Glass with its darkened edge, specular rim and top sheen, and reserve it for chrome.
- **Do** group related toolbar actions in one glass capsule, with the prominent one as the Graphite segment.
- **Do** give each sidebar place its own icon hue, light and dark, and keep the row's selection a grey pill.
- **Do** let a page name itself with a 28px bold large title or the sidebar selection. The toolbar holds only the menu button (phone), the activity view, and the glass capsules.
- **Do** use the graphite-grey selection with white text only while a table has focus, and the quiet grey tint otherwise.
- **Do** use SF Mono only for code and data, with tabular numerals.
- **Do** write section labels at 11px semibold in sentence case.
- **Do** give a canvas the full width and float its panes and controls over it as Liquid Glass; pan to reveal a stage, never re-zoom.
- **Do** draw wires in wire grey, turning ink only for the selected stage or node.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use system blue, orange or brown anywhere: not as an accent, a link, a selection, a focus ring, a status, a node colour or a place hue.
- **Don't** set yellow text on a light window.
- **Don't** use lane colours or place hues for state, action or selection.
- **Don't** colour a wire by lane, or a link inside one graph by node hue.
- **Don't** put a page title in the toolbar.
- **Don't** inset or round the sidebar into a floating pane.
- **Don't** let the desktop fields show anywhere but through the sidebar glass, or make content panes, tables or grouped boxes translucent.
- **Don't** use tracked uppercase labels or mono eyebrows.
- **Don't** outline badges, or add borders or shadows to grouped boxes.
- **Don't** load web fonts. The system stack is the typeface.
- **Don't** use aurora gradients, glowing dots, or floating cards on a tinted field in content. The only gradients are the desktop fields behind the sidebar and the sheen on glass.

## Known Limits

- The backend's shared SQLite connection can return malformed rows under concurrent requests, seen as a 500 on /api/sources. This is a pre-existing backend defect, not a design rule.
